About Me

I am a Robotics Systems graduate from the Singapore Institute of Technology specializing in industrial automation, control systems, and system integration. I have experience in developing automation systems using PLCs, industrial robots, and sensor-actuator integration.

Over the past year, I worked as a Control Engineer at SLS Bearings Pte Ltd, where I developed control logic and performed system integration for industrial automation systems involving PLCs, HMI systems, and robotic arms. I was responsible for programming PLC control sequences using the CODESYS platform, designing HMI interfaces for system operation and monitoring, and integrating sensors and actuators to deliver a fully functional automation system meeting project specifications.

Throughout my undergraduate studies at Singapore Institute of Technology, I led project teams to complete various robotics projects. One of my key projects involves the autonomous vehicle AgileX Limo Pro, where the project team is responsible for ensuring the vehicle is able to navigate in an environment full of obstacles. My biggest contribution was the programming of the AgileX Limo Pro using the Robotic Operating System software platform to perform waypoint navigation with obstacle avoidance.
